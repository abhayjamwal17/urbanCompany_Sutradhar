Three steps. Then it runs.

Most of planning isn't hard — there's just too much of it to hold in your head at once.

1

Answer six questions

Date, city, venue, guest count, budget, and who approves spending. Once. That's the whole setup.

2

Forward vendor quotes

Text, photo or voice note — in any language. Sutradhar reads it and tells you what it says.

3

Approve. It does the rest.

Payments raised, deliveries booked, vendors updated. You see everything in one running ledger.

What it actually does

Not inspiration boards or vendor listings. The administrative work that nobody wants.

🎙

Understands voice notes

Your decorator sends a Hindi voice note quoting "बयालीस हज़ार रुपये". Sutradhar reads it as ₹42,000 and checks it against your budget.

₹

Raises staged payments

Advance now, balance on delivery. Secure payment links, and a ledger of who's been paid what.

📦

Books and tracks deliveries

Props from Jaipur, flowers from Pune. Serviceability checked before anything is booked.

💬

Talks to vendors for you

In their language, on WhatsApp, by text or voice note. They don't sign up for anything.

What it will never do without asking you

It handles your money, so the limits are strict and built in:

  • Never confirms a price with a vendor — you approve every amount first
  • Never raises a payment for an amount you haven't approved
  • Never books a delivery without checking both pincodes are serviceable
  • Never messages your guests — invitations stay yours

Vendors, you're covered too

No app, no listing fee, no sign-up. Keep working on WhatsApp exactly as you do now — and stop chasing clients for payment. Sutradhar sends the reminders so you don't have to.
